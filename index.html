📋 Which supporting documents are customary?

Depending on the scheme, these are the most frequently requested documents:

  • Manufacturer's declaration with Ug value — mandatory for most subsidy schemes.
  • Supplier sheet / datasheet — technical sheet of the glass.
  • Glass Warranty — product warranty and installation warranty.
  • Invoice — clearly indicating the type of glass and Ug.
  • Before/after photos — proof of the existing condition.
  • EPB report / proof of performance — in the case of new construction or renovation.
